Sweet Arrows

Tap, slide, pop the sweets

Tap a candy and it slides in the direction of its arrow until it bumps into something. Line up three or more of a color and they pop. New candies keep arriving, faster and faster: keep the board from filling up and chase your best score. No dragging: just tap, slide and pop.

Support

Questions, an idea, or lost progress? Mail support@sweetarrowsgame.com. In the app, Settings → Contact opens a mail with your app version already filled in.

Fair ads

Sweet Arrows is free and paid for by ads. You choose when to watch a rewarded video. Interstitial ads only start after a few rounds, at most one every two rounds, never during a round. Remove Ads or VIP turns them off.
