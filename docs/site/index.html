Sweet Arrows

Tap, slide, pop the sweets

Tap a candy and it shoots off in the direction of its arrow. A clear path sends it into the candy jar, three alike pop, and a candy in the way makes it bounce back. Clear the board before the jar fills up. No timers, no dragging: just you, the arrows and a jar of five slots.

Support

Questions, a level that feels unfair, or lost progress? Mail support@sweetarrowsgame.com. In the app, Settings → Contact opens a mail with your app version already filled in.

Fair ads

Sweet Arrows is free and paid for by ads. You choose when to watch a rewarded video. Interstitial ads only start at level 8, at most one every two levels, never during a level. Remove Ads or VIP turns them off.
