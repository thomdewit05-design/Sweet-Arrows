Sweet Arrows

Tap, slide, pop the sweets

Tap a candy and it slides in the direction of its arrow until it bumps into something. Line up three of a color and they pop. New candies keep arriving, faster and faster, with livelier music as you go: keep the board from filling up and chase your best score. Level up, finish daily missions and unlock new backdrops. No dragging: just tap, slide and pop.

Support

Questions, an idea, or lost progress? Mail support@sweetarrowsgame.com. In the app, Settings → Contact opens a mail with your app version already filled in.

Fair ads

Sweet Arrows is free and paid for by ads. You choose when to watch a rewarded video. Full-screen ads only come between rounds, never during one, and at most one every two rounds. There are no in-app purchases.
