Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Sweet Arrows ("the app") is made by Luc-Thomas de Wit, the Netherlands ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. There are no accounts: we never ask for your name, email or password, and the app has no in-app purchases.

What the app stores on your device and in your iCloud

Your progress (best score, level, coins, boosters, daily missions, settings) is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage so it moves with you to a new iPhone. We cannot see this data.

Advertising

The app is free and paid for by ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, now and then a full-screen ad between rounds (never during a round), and optional videos you choose to watch for a reward. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we first ask for your consent with Google's consent form. On iOS we also ask, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used. If you decline, you see non-personalised ads and can still play everything. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings → Privacy settings, and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google may process device information, the advertising identifier (only with permission), IP address and ad interactions. See how Google uses data.

Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand whether the game is too hard or too easy (for example "round started / ended with score 1200"), and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. This data is tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to you as a person.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and best scores are shared with Apple's Game Center service under your Game Center profile.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for the Apple Kids category.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data. Because we do not hold accounts, most data lives with Apple and Google; deleting the app and its iCloud data removes your progress. For questions mail support@sweetarrowsgame.com. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

We will update this page when the app's use of data changes.
